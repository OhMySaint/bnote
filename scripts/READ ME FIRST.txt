BNote 0.2.0 — preview build

INSTALL
1. Drag BNote into the Applications folder.
2. First launch: RIGHT-CLICK BNote -> "Open" -> click "Open" again.
   (The preview is signed but not notarised by Apple, so macOS warns once.)
   Still blocked? Open Terminal and run:
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/BNote.app

REQUIRES: macOS 15 or later.

YOUR PAGES stay on this Mac, in
~/Library/Containers/com.hoangson.BNote/
Installing a newer BNote over the old one never touches them, and the app
keeps the last 5 store snapshots in that folder under "Backups".
